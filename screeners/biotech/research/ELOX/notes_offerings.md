## 5. Pipeline, milestones, runway

**Pipeline** [S-1 Sep, Prospectus Summary; Business]
- **Exaluren** (ribosome-selective glycoside, PTC readthrough). Orphan Drug Designation for Alport syndrome. Evaluated in 8 trials, 145 subjects dosed, 89.4 subject-months.
- Phase 2a NMAS (UK, Jan 2023 to Apr 2024, **3 patients**, COL4A4): primary endpoint safety; reduction in podocyte foot process effacement in all 3; filtration slit density up 50%; **UPCR decreased in 1 patient and increased in 2** at end of treatment.
- **ZKN-013** licensed to **Almirall** (March 2024): $3M upfront + $3M milestone received in 2024; eligible for up to **~$470.0 million** milestones plus tiered royalties; Almirall may terminate for convenience.
