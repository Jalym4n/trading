## 5. Pipeline and stated milestones

- **Exaluren, Phase 2b in nonsense-mutation Alport syndrome (NMAS):**
  - IND cleared by FDA ([Q2] MD&A Overview)
  - **Initiation "in the third quarter of 2026"** ([Q2] MD&A; [PR])
  - Topline from the 16-week placebo-controlled portion **by mid-2027**, final readout **by end of 2027** ([PR]; also [Q1] MD&A). [Q2] MD&A does not restate topline timing
  - Design per [10-K] Business: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, delayed-start; ~24 patients aged 12+ with COL4A3/4/5 nonsense mutations; 2:1 randomization; 16 weeks then crossover; 16 more weeks
- **Timing history for Phase 2b initiation:** "first half of 2026" ([10-K] Business; [Q1] MD&A), then "third quarter of 2026" ([Q2] MD&A; [PR]).
- **Prior Phase 2a data** (n=3, Jan 2023 to Apr 2024, UK): all three showed reduced podocyte foot process effacement on TEM. "At the end of treatment, UPCR had decreased for one patient and increased for two patients" ([Q2] MD&A Overview).
- **Exaluren, nmADPKD:** preclinical. Phase 2 enrollment planned **in 2027**, after protocol finalization and IND clearance ([Q2] MD&A). Topline **by mid-2028** ([PR]; [Q1] MD&A).
- **Regulatory designations:**
  - **July 2026:** European Commission orphan medicinal product designation for exaluren in ADPKD (EU/3/26/3273) ([Q2] MD&A; [PR])
  - FDA orphan drug designation for AS ([Q2] MD&A)
- **ZKN-013** (licensed to Almirall for RDEB/JEB):
  - Received $3M upfront plus a $3M milestone in 2024
  - Eligible for up to **~$470.0M** in milestones plus tiered royalties
  - Almirall declined continued R&D services in March 2025
  - No revenue in Q1 or Q2 2026 ([Q2] Note 6)
  - Domicilium is entitled to a low-thirties then mid-twenties percentage of milestone payments, capped in the "mid-double-digit millions" ([Q2] Note 6, Royalty Agreement)
- No clinical data readouts were reported in Q1 or Q2 2026 ([Q1], [Q2], [PR]).
