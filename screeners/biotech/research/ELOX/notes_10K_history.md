**Exaluren clinical facts**
- 8 trials, **145 subjects** dosed; "no dose-limiting toxicities were reported, and no serious adverse events were attributed to exaluren" (10-K Item 1).
- Phase 2a: FSD rose **50%, 13.3% and 116%** (mean 50%); Collagen IV alpha 3/4 rose **38.5%, 8.3%, 170.8%**; UPCR fell ~37% in one patient and "nominally increased in the other 2 patients" (10-K Item 1 Phase 2a).
- CF and cystinosis Phase 2 results "highlighted the need for higher tissue exposures to translate biological activity to clinical efficacy" (10-K Item 1).
- Phase 2b design: ~**24** patients, 2:1 randomization, 16 weeks blinded then delayed start, primary endpoint FSD on biopsy for adults and non-US pediatrics. **FDA "did not support the use of protocol-mandated repeat kidney biopsies in pediatric patients"** in the US, so US pediatric primary objective is safety. "FSD has not been validated as a surrogate endpoint for clinical benefit in AS" (10-K Item 1 Phase 2b).
- Orphan drug designation: CF, cystinosis, MPS I, AS, Rett syndrome (10-K Risk Factor Summary).

**Trial IDs (NCT):** **not stated** in the 10-K. Only NCT number in the reviewed set: **NCT04135495**, the earlier ELX-02 Phase 2 CF study (G542X), cited as a loan "Clinical Milestone" (Ex. 10.32 definitions). No NCT for the Phase 2b NMAS trial in these documents.

**Patents:** 66 issued, 101 pending (2025-12-31). Exaluren composition-of-matter (Technion in-license) **expires 2031**; synthesis, ocular-use and Rett-use families 2038 (10-K Item 1 Intellectual Property).

## 5. Company-specific risk factors (10-K Item 1A)

- **Filing delinquency:** "there are limited examples of companies successfully completing such a process."
- **Registration revocation:** "If the SEC brought an administrative action against us, it is likely that we would cease being a public company."
- **No shelf for a year:** "we will not be eligible to register the offer and sale of our securities using a shelf registration statement on Form S-3 until we have timely filed all periodic reports required under the Exchange Act for one year."
- **Uplisting:** "we may face heightened scrutiny from Nasdaq as a result of our history of being unable to meet Nasdaq's listing requirements."
- **No trial funding:** "if we are unable to raise sufficient funds, we will not be able to conduct our planned Phase 2b clinical trial."
- **Single-asset dependency:** "We are heavily dependent on favorable efficacy results from this study in the near term for our continued development and funding for exaluren and the Company."
- **Open-label Phase 2a:** "data from our Phase 2a clinical trial of exaluren in AS patients may not be predictive of data from our planned clinical trials for exaluren, which will be blinded."
- **Almirall control:** "Almirall has control and broad discretion over all aspects of the development and commercialization of ZKN-013 and we will have little, if any, influence."
- **Investor consent rights:** "These rights could discourage a takeover of the company, could limit our ability to raise additional funds... and could result in Coastland and Domicilium controlling the composition of our Board of Directors."
- **Single-source supply:** "Given this reliance on a single-source supplier, we are especially susceptible to supply shortages because we do not have alternate suppliers currently available."
- **Headcount:** "As of December 31, 2025, we had ten full-time employees"; three are in Israel.
- **Israel exposure:** "our insurance does not cover losses that may occur as a result of an event associated with the security situation in the Middle East."
- **IIA grants:** transfer of IIA-supported know-how abroad may cost "up to a maximum of six times the amount of grants received."
- **Patent life:** exaluren composition-of-matter patents "are currently set to expire in 2031."
- **Penny stock:** "Our securities have in the past constituted, and may again in the future constitute, 'penny stock'."
- **Material weakness:** "the material weakness has not been remediated."
- **NOLs:** Israeli NOLs of **$126.2M**; federal NOLs "could materially decrease" under Section 382.

## 8. Surprises and inconsistencies across documents

1. **Pre-funded warrant counts do not reconcile.** Note 9 (38,221,656 PFW at year end) plus Note 16 (16,326,529 issued in 2026) gives **54,548,185**. Item 12 says **52,507,369** at 2026-03-16. The **2,040,816** gap equals the Feb 2026 debt-exchange warrant, which Item 1A, Item 5 and Item 12 appear to omit. Shares outstanding rose only 281,696 in the period, so exercise does not explain it.
2. **Coastlands total:** 30,612,243 (Item 5, Item 7) vs **30,615,243** (Item 1A).
3. **Board vs. contract.** The SPA amendments require board reconstitution with a Coastlands designee and a Domicilium representative at the Third Tranche Closing (Ex. 10.50 Sec. 5.11) or designation rights (Ex. 10.51 Sec. 5.11). The 10-K, filed after that closing, lists Aggarwal, Walts and Rubin and does not name any designee (10-K Item 10).
4. **Covenant compliance vs. defaults.** 10-K: "in compliance with all debt covenants as of December 31, 2025" (Item 7). Ex. 10.34 (May 2025) acknowledges "certain Defaults and Events of Default existing". Maturity was 2025-04-01 and the extension is not documented.
5. **CFF Omnibus condition missed, then retro-amended.** The original Omnibus voided the royalty cut unless **$15M** was raised by **2025-12-15** (Ex. 10.38 Sec. 5). Only $10M was raised by 2025-12-12. On **2026-02-26** the deadline was moved to 2026-03-31, "effective as of October 28, 2025" (Ex. 10.38 Amendment).
6. **Third Tranche deadline.** Note 1 and Note 9 give "no later than February 20, 2026"; Item 7 and Note 16 give March 13, 2026. Note 16 dates the extending amendment **2026-02-26**; Ex. 10.51 is dated **2026-02-20**.
